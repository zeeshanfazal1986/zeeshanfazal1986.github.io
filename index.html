About Me

I am a Bioinformatics Scientist specializing in clinical genomics and multi-omics data integration, currently working as a Computational Genomics Specialist at NIH/NIAID through Guidehouse Digital. My work spans the full analytical stack — from raw sequencing data through variant calling, annotation, transcriptomics, and biological interpretation — across 8+ concurrent research programs in immunology, rare disease, and infectious disease.

I hold a PhD in Animal Sciences with a Bioinformatics concentration from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where I also completed my MS in Bioinformatics. Prior to my NIH work, I was a Bioinformatics Scientist at Fate Therapeutics (San Diego) supporting CAR-T and NK cell immunotherapy programs, and a postdoctoral researcher at both UIUC and Stanford University.

My research has produced 18+ peer-reviewed publications (h-index 14, 818 citations) spanning cancer genomics, clinical immunology, epigenetics, and computational biology. I am a recipient of the NASA Group Achievement Award and an active member of ASHG and AACR.
