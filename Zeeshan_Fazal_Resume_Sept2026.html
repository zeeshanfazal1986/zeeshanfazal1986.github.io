Professional Summary

Bioinformatics PhD with 9+ years of experience in clinical genomics, transcriptomics, and multi-omics integration across immunology, rare disease, cancer, and infectious disease research — spanning NIH/NIAID, Fate Therapeutics, Stanford University, and UIUC. Deep expertise in WGS/WES somatic and germline variant calling (GATK, Mutect2), bulk and single-cell RNA-seq (including viral infection and immune response contexts), and scalable HPC pipeline development (Snakemake, SLURM). Consistent record of independently leading multi-cohort analyses and translating results into peer-reviewed publications (h-index 14, 818 citations, 18+ papers). Proficient in AI-assisted development with GitHub and Claude Code.
